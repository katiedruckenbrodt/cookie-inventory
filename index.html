Troop Cookie Inventory — ABC Bakers

🍪 tracked with help from Binx the lop-eared rabbit

A small FastAPI app for a 27-scout ABC Bakers troop to track cookie stock, scout checkouts, booth sales, payments, and who owes what — all computed live from a SQLite ledger, nothing hardcoded.

Dashboard

Boxes on hand per variety, outstanding amount owed, and total cash collected.

Scouts

Roster of 27 scouts with boxes checked out and amount owed per scout.

Cookie Varieties

The ABC Bakers lineup (Thin Mints, Caramel deLites, Adventurefuls, and more) with editable per-box pricing.

Inventory & Sales

Receive stock, check boxes out to a scout, log returns, record booth sales, and take payments.

Full Ledger

Every transaction ever recorded, newest first — the source of truth behind every figure on the site.

Run it locally

pip install -r requirements.txt
uvicorn cookie_inventory_app:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000

Data lives in data/cookie_inventory.db (SQLite, git-ignored). The database is created and seeded automatically on first run.
